The array feeds the daytime share of the load directly and refills the bank from empty within the recharge target, with the grid or a generator carrying the night load meanwhile. Cloudy days aren't modelled: lower the performance ratio or raise the recharge target to allow for them.

Estimates for planning. Preset watts, power factor and Start × values are typical figures, so use nameplate values where you have them. Confirm surge ratings, cell chemistry limits and maximum charge and discharge currents with the battery and inverter datasheets before quoting.
